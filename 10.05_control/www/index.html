DEVICES

      

      

      

      

      
Switches act on the running emulation, like typing en, dis and
        p … at the D>>> prompt (HALT, INIT and RESTART send h 1/h 0,
        init and pwr). They do not change the saved configuration;
        what demo does with them shows in the console.

    

  


  
  

    

      
MY CONFIGURATIONS

      

      

        
        
      

      
A configuration says which controllers and drives to emulate. The panel turns it into a
        demo --cmdfile command file, shown as a printout below the editor.

    

    

      

        
Select a configuration, or press NEW.

        
Tip: the LIBRARY lists the stock scripts from files.retrocmp.com; any of them can be copied into an editable configuration.
